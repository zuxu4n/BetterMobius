# BetterMobius privacy policy

*Last updated: September 30, 2026*

BetterMobius does not collect, store, transmit or share any data.

- **No data collection.** It doesn't read or record personal information,
  browsing history, course content, answers or anything you type.
- **No storage.** It keeps nothing between visits. Settings like playback
  speed reset each time a page loads, and drawings and calculator entries are
  thrown away when you close them.
- **Nothing sent anywhere.** The only thing it downloads is each slide's
  narration audio, from the same Möbius page that's already playing it, to
  find the pauses between sentences. That analysis happens in your browser,
  and nothing about it leaves your computer.
- **No permissions.** It runs only on `mobius.cloud` pages and requests no
  browser permissions.
- **No analytics, ads or third parties.**

BetterMobius is an unofficial tool and is not affiliated with DigitalEd or
Möbius.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/zuxu4n/BetterMobius/issues.
