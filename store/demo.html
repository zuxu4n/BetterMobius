2.1 Supply and Demand

The law of demand

  • As price rises, quantity demanded falls
  • Holding everything else constant
  • A change in price moves along the curve
  • Other changes shift the whole curve
1:12 / 3:30

Demand describes how much of a good buyers are willing and able to purchase at each price. The demand curve slopes downward: at lower prices, buyers purchase more.
